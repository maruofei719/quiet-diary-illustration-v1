# Quiet Diary Illustration

*A small place for photographs that wanted to become memories.*

---

Transform ordinary photographs into quiet watercolor diary illustrations.

Inspired by sketchbooks, travel journals, paper textures, and handwritten memories.

---

## 🌿 Overview

Quiet Diary Illustration transforms ordinary photographs into poetic watercolor diary pages.

Instead of recreating reality, it preserves the feeling that remains after the moment has passed.

---

## ✨ Features

- Watercolor illustration
- Hand-drawn paper texture
- Five handwriting personalities
- Poetic bilingual quotes
- Original aspect ratio preserved
